What it actually does

  1. Gathers. Federal grants, contributions and CanadaBuys tenders, pulled together and kept current, alongside sources that don’t reach the big portals at all.
  2. Matches. Filtered against your capabilities, your region and your certifications — so the list stays short enough to read.
  3. Explains. Every match says why it fits. When something nearly fits but doesn’t, it says that too, instead of quietly padding the list.

Not a keyword alert

Keyword feeds return everything containing a word. This returns what you can win.

Short on purpose

A handful of real matches beats five hundred results you’ll never open.

Built where it’s used

Indigenous-owned, built in Ohsweken, for the businesses it serves.

Already listed in a supplier directory?

Good — keep that listing. Directories and certification registries do the buyer’s half of the job: they help departments and prime contractors find Indigenous suppliers. That matters, and Ohahase doesn’t replace any of it.

This is the other half. Being findable doesn’t tell you that a solicitation you could win closed last Thursday. Ohahase watches the other direction — from your business out to the opportunities — so the two work together rather than against each other.

Who’s behind it
